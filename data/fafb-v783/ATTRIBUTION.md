# FlyWire FAFB v783 data

This directory contains independently generated browser artifacts derived from the FlyWire FAFB public Codex v783 exports.

- Source: https://codex.flywire.ai/
- Source files: https://storage.googleapis.com/flywire-data/codex/data/fafb/783/
- License: [Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/)
- Modifications: connectivity is aggregated by directed neuron pair, assigned stable numeric indices, encoded as typed sparse arrays, and joined with classification, representative-coordinate, cell-type, and optic-lobe column annotations. Functional role predicates and neural-model signs are project modeling choices.

No endorsement by FlyWire or the cited authors is implied.
